One element that often exists in portal applications is an entertainment center, most notably games. On platforms like Facebook and Yahoo, the available games range from casual arcade games to fully fledged MMOs. Typically, those games are developed by third-party companies that opt for their games to be published on the platforms.

For the purpose of ambience and as a social catalyst, MyBiasWorld may also provide a collection of games for the platform. For simplicity, the games will be self-developed instead of using those developed by third parties. The games should lean toward simple, casual games that either value daily play or multiplayer incentives.

Example of games to be added:

- Multiplayer
  - Chess (PvP/PvC, 1v1, random matchmaking/direct invitation)
  - Billiard (PvP/PvC, 1v1, random matchmaking/direct invitation)
  - Monopoly (PvP/PvC, 4 max players, random matchmaking/direct invitation)
  - Poker (PvP/PvC, 7 max players, join available room)
- Singleplayer
  - Leaning to daily and repeatable games such as Wordle, Sudoku, Minesweeper, Crossword, Spelling Bee, 2048, and other puzzle and word games.

The multiplayer games should feature a chat and spectator system.

:::note
Since the games are mostly for decorative purpose, it shouldn't be the primary focus of development. The games may stay simple as long as they are functioning. For example, the chess computer doesn't have to be a smart AI and the billiard game doesn't need a ranking system.
:::
